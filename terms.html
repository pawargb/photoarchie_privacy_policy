2. What PhotoArchie does

PhotoArchie accesses the Apple Photos items you permit it to use and creates smaller, local photo and video copies for browsing. These copies may have lower resolution, quality, or metadata than the originals. An item that has not been compressed may still be displayed from Apple Photos while it is available there.

PhotoArchie is not a full replacement for Apple Photos, an original-quality archive, a cloud backup service, or an encrypted vault. Features may depend on Photos permission, available device storage, iOS behavior, and whether an original is available on the device.
